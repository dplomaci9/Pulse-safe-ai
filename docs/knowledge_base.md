|---|---|---|

| SDAIA, AI Ethics Principles (English), Sept 2023 | https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/wcm/connect/4c56ed1c-1b82-447d-ac29-638f5f99c12e/ai-principles-EN.pdf | Fairness, privacy & security, humanity, social benefit, reliability & safety, transparency, accountability |
| SDAIA, Personal Data Protection Law and implementing regulation | https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Pages/RegulationsAndPolicies.aspx | Lawful processing, data-subject rights, governance, retention, protection obligations |

| WHO, Ethics and governance of artificial intelligence for health | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240029200 | Autonomy, safety, transparency, accountability, equity, governance for health AI |
| NIST, AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 | https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework | Risk identification, measurement, management, monitoring, lifecycle governance |
| ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI — Guidance on risk management | https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html | Structured AI risk-management activities |
| ISO 14971:2019 — Medical devices — Application of risk management | https://www.iso.org/standard/72704.html | Future reference for medical-device risk management; not a compliance claim at prototype stage |
| IEC 62304 — Medical device software — Software life cycle processes | https://www.iso.org/standard/38421.html | Future software-lifecycle reference if the concept proceeds toward regulated clinical software |
